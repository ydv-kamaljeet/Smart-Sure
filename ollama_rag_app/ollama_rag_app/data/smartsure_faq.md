# SmartSure — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is SmartSure?
SmartSure is an online insurance management platform that allows users to purchase vehicle and home insurance policies, file and track claims, and manage their insurance portfolio. Administrators can manage users, policies, claims, and generate reports.

## How do I buy a policy?
1. Log in to your SmartSure account.
2. Click "Buy Policy" from the sidebar menu.
3. Choose the insurance type: Vehicle or Home.
4. Select a specific plan (e.g., Third Party, Comprehensive, Basic Home, Premium Home).
5. Enter the required details (vehicle info or home details).
6. Review the calculated premium and IDV (Insured Declared Value).
7. Confirm the purchase. Your policy is now active.

## How is the premium calculated?

### Vehicle Insurance
- IDV = Listed Price × (1 - Depreciation based on vehicle age)
- Annual Premium = IDV × 2%
- If annual mileage exceeds 15,000 km, a 20% surcharge is added.

Depreciation rates by vehicle age:
- Less than 1 year: 5%
- 1-2 years: 15%
- 2-3 years: 20%
- 3-4 years: 30%
- 4-5 years: 40%
- 5+ years: 50%

### Home Insurance
- IDV = Property Value × (1 - Depreciation based on building age)
- Annual Premium = IDV × 0.1%
- If the home has a security system, a 10% discount applies.

Depreciation rates by building age:
- Less than 5 years: 0%
- 5-10 years: 10%
- 10-20 years: 20%
- 20-30 years: 30%
- 30+ years: 40%

## How do I file a claim?
1. Go to "Initiate Claim" from the sidebar.
2. Select the policy you want to file a claim against. Only active policies are eligible.
3. Enter the incident date, description, and claim amount.
4. The claim amount cannot exceed the policy's IDV (Insured Declared Value).
5. Upload supporting documents if required.
6. Submit the claim.

## What are the claim statuses?
- **Submitted**: Claim has been filed and is waiting for review.
- **Under Review / In Review**: An administrator is currently reviewing the claim.
- **Approved**: The claim has been approved, and the payout will be processed.
- **Rejected**: The claim was denied. You should receive an email with the reason.

## Can I cancel my policy?
Users cannot cancel their own policies. Only administrators can cancel a policy. If you need a policy cancelled, please contact the SmartSure support team or your administrator.

## What happens when my policy is cancelled?
- The policy status changes to "Cancelled".
- You will receive an email notification about the cancellation.
- Any pending claims on a cancelled policy will be blocked.
- No new claims can be filed against a cancelled policy.

## Can I file a claim on a cancelled policy?
No. Claims can only be filed against policies with an "Active" status. If your policy has been cancelled, the system will reject the claim submission.

## What file types can I upload for claim documents?
You can upload common document formats. The documents are securely stored in MEGA Cloud Storage.

## How do I reset my password?
1. Go to the login page.
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. You will receive an OTP (One-Time Password) via email.
5. Enter the OTP to verify your identity.
6. Set your new password.

## Can I log in with Google?
Yes. SmartSure supports Google OAuth 2.0 login. Click the "Sign in with Google" button on the login page.

## Who can access the Admin panel?
Only users with the "Admin" role can access the Admin panel. The admin portal provides dashboards, user management, policy management, claims management, audit logs, system logs, and report generation features.

## What reports can admins generate?
Admins can generate:
- Policy Reports (CSV and PDF)
- Claims Reports (CSV and PDF)
- User Reports
These reports include summary statistics and detailed records.
